01 // Introduction

What is a processor?

A processor is the "brain" of a computer. It fetches instructions from memory, decodes them, and executes them: arithmetic, logic, and data movement billions of times every second.

What it is

The CPU (central processing unit) runs program instructions. Modern chips are often a full SoC, adding graphics, AI engines, and memory controllers beside the CPU cores.

Why it matters

Every phone, laptop, car, server and game console depends on one. Faster, more efficient processors made the internet, smartphones and modern AI possible.

How it evolved

Thousands of transistors became billions; one core became many; and general-purpose chips gained specialised engines for graphics and AI.
